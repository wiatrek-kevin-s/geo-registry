**Status: proposals only. Nothing here is reviewed until the Texas steward signs off.**
An agent researched each Texas data entry on 2026-11-05, tested what it could reach and
drafted overrides (`seed/overrides/<id>.yaml`, marked PROPOSED). `review.state` stays
`seeded` on every entry, so Columbus still shows them as leads.

How to approve: for each row, tick it (or change the decision) in the pull request. When a
row is approved, the override gets `review: {state: reviewed, reviewed_by: <steward>,
reviewed_on: <date>}`, and "To check" items are resolved first.

"Tested" means the endpoint was fetched and answered as described. The agent could only
fetch addresses that a web search returned, so some endpoints are marked "not yet tested";
the weekly Health workflow (or the steward) tests those.

Questions for the steward:
1. **Licences.** Most agencies state no licence, only a disclaimer. Texas public information is
   generally open, but the registry should not say "public domain" without the publisher saying
   so. Proposal: keep each entry's current licence value until checked, and record disclaimers in
   `notes`.
2. **TxGIO products.** Keep TOP, lidar and StratMap as their own entries (each has its own
   services) linked to txgio-datahub, or merge them into it as collections?
3. **Personal data.** TCAD and StratMap parcels carry owner names. Columbus's default policy may
   want to hide them from AI providers (design set, open question "Field hiding").

Out of this batch (national or global; later batches): fema-nfhl (already reviewed separately),
geofabrik-downloads, national-address-database, blm-navigator-plss-cadnsdi.

## railroad-commission-of-texas-gis
- https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/data-sets-available-for-download/ fetched:
  free shapefiles by county, updated twice a week: Pipeline, Survey, Well, Base and All layers
  (mft.rrc.texas.gov/link/... download links), plus statewide API data (ASCII, dBase).
- IMPORTANT: coordinates are "Latitude/Longitude Decimal Degrees, NAD 27" (a .prj is included):
  a pitfall for anyone combining them with NAD 83 data.
- Terms: conversion and software compatibility are the user's responsibility; RRC disclaimer
  (via Travis County's copy): informational only, no accuracy claim.
- Download links: Pipeline https://mft.rrc.texas.gov/link/c7cbab0c-afe2-4f6f-91ae-e6ed7d3a7ab6,
  Survey https://mft.rrc.texas.gov/link/7bee2b33-4d7c-45a6-b3bc-61215f214c3c,
  Well https://mft.rrc.texas.gov/link/d551fb20-442e-4b67-84fa-ac3f23ecabb4.
- The GIS Viewer (entry's URL) is interactive only.
